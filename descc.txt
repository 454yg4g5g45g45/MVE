[b][u]MOD REQUIREMENTS & SETUP[/u][/b]

[b]Game Version[/b]  
• Base Rome 2.0.5
• English language selected.

[b]Keyboard Requirements[/b]  
• Must use an [b]English keyboard layout[/b]:  
  • US (English)  
  • UK (English)  
  • Australia (English) etc.
• You may keep your main keyboard layout, but ensure an English layout is added and active.  
• [b]Do NOT use non-English keyboard mapping[/b] — they cause input and script issues.  
• Make sure no non-English characters are being mapped.

[b]Windows 10/11 Settings[/b]  
• [b]Daylight Savings Time: DISABLED[/b]

[b]Rome Remastered Preferences[/b]  
Inside your preferences file, ensure the following is set:  
• [b]BATTERY_SAVER:FALSE[/b]

[b]Required Cleanup for Reinstall/Playing[/b]  
• Delete the old MVE mod folder located at:  
  XYZ:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\workshop\content\885970\[b]3575143236[/b]
• Delete your entire cache/settings folder
  C:\Users\YOU\AppData\Local\Feral Interactive
(This forces the game to rebuild clean settings.)

[h1]Important![/h1]
Mod is made to be played on [u][b]unit variation ON[/b][/u], [u][b]realistic color scheme ON[/b][/u], and any difficulty is fine.[u][b].[/b][/u]

[u][b]This mod is a vast total overhaul for Rome Remastered, mainly using Kirsi's Even Bigger Map for the 306 BC Imperial Campaign, new units, new character names, new cultures, new subcultures, etc. and nearly 347 new factions and counting, 1561 regions, 28 of them playable.[/b][/u]

[list]
[*][b]Scripted Content & Events[/b] – Experience a wide array of dynamic, historical, alternate history, fantastical, and mythological challenges.  
[*][b]Survival & Crisis Management[/b] – Endure revolts, hordes, civil unrest, and the rise of emergent, remnant, and re-emergent nations.
[*][b]Administration & Colonial System[/b] – Develop and maintain your empire or grant independence to a vassal via a building for monetary gain.
[*][b]Renegade and Revolting Factions[/b] – Experience the rise of new factions and utilization of “Crisis” content from GoRR and TC 1.0 with a focus on revolts and rebellions
[*][b]Gigantic Building System[/b] – Conditional paths for a large swath of buildings that can be used for military, economic, social, agricultural, religious, etc. purposes that is always updated with new additions.
[*][b]Various Pseudo Mechanics via Buildings[/b] – Develop and respond to various WIP, Currently Added, and planned pseudo mechanics.
[/list]
